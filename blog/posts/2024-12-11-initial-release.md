# Introducing Video Speed Controller

Tired of videos that are too slow? Or need to slow down a tutorial to follow along? Video Speed Controller gives you precise control over playback speed on any website.

## What It Does

Video Speed Controller is a Chrome extension that works on YouTube, Vimeo, Netflix, and virtually any site with HTML5 video. Use simple keyboard shortcuts to:

- **Speed up** – Press `D` to increase speed
- **Slow down** – Press `A` to decrease speed
- **Reset** – Press `S` to return to normal (1×) speed

The extension cycles through a range of speeds from 0.05× (super slow-mo) to 16× (lightning fast), with sensible steps in between.

## Why I Built This

Existing speed controllers felt clunky or didn't work reliably across sites. I wanted something that just works – no fiddling, no configuration required out of the box, but fully customisable if you want it.

## Getting Started

1. Install from the Chrome Web Store (or load unpacked for development)
2. Navigate to any page with a video
3. Press `D` to speed up, `A` to slow down, `S` to reset

A subtle overlay appears briefly to show your current speed, then fades away.

## What's Next

This first release covers the basics. Coming soon: customisable shortcuts, on-screen controls, and more speed presets.
