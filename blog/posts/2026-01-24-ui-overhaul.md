# New Look: On-Screen Controls and In-Page Settings

Big update! Video Speed Controller now has optional on-screen controls and you can access settings right from the page you're watching.

## On-Screen Speed Buttons

Not everyone wants to memorise keyboard shortcuts. The new on-screen controls appear in the corner of the video and let you:
- See your current speed at a glance
- Click to speed up or slow down
- Access settings without leaving the page

Enable them in settings – they're off by default to keep things minimal.

## In-Page Settings Modal

Previously, changing settings meant clicking the extension icon and using a separate popup. Now you can click the ⚙️ button on the on-screen controls to open settings right there, overlaid on the page. Change your speeds, update shortcuts, toggle options – all without interrupting your video.

## Pause-to-Reset Option

New toggle: automatically reset to 1× speed when you pause a video. Useful if you speed through content but want to resume at normal pace. Find it in settings.

## Keyboard Shortcut Hints

The on-screen buttons now show which keys to press. Helpful while you're learning the shortcuts – once you've got muscle memory, you can disable hints in settings.

## How to Enable

1. Click the extension icon or press `Cmd+Shift+V` (Mac) / `Ctrl+Shift+V` (Windows)
2. Toggle "Show on-screen controls"
3. Optional: Enable "Show keyboard hints"
