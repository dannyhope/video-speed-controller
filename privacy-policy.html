Privacy Policy — Video Speed Controller

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Video Speed Controller is a browser extension that changes HTML5 video playback speed on websites you visit. This policy explains what the extension does with your data.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any server operated by the extension developer. The extension runs in your browser. There is no developer-operated backend, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

What happens in your browser

  • The extension detects video elements (and some YouTube embed iframes) on pages you open so it can change playback rate and show on-screen controls.
  • Your settings (custom speeds, keyboard shortcuts, and related preferences) are stored using Chrome’s sync storage so they can follow your Chrome profile if sync is enabled.
  • On pages with YouTube embeds, the extension may load YouTube’s IFrame Player API in the page context so playback rate can be set on those embeds. That request goes to YouTube/Google, not to the developer.

Permissions

  • storage — save and sync your settings.
  • sidePanel — open settings from the on-video settings button.
  • Host access (content scripts on pages you visit) — required so speed controls work on videos across websites. The extension does not read page content for advertising or sell data.

Third parties

  • Google Chrome — settings sync via Chrome storage when you use Chrome Sync.
  • YouTube / Google — only when controlling YouTube embeds via the IFrame Player API.

Data retention

Settings remain in your browser (and Chrome Sync, if enabled) until you clear them or uninstall the extension. The developer does not retain a copy.

Children

This extension is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
