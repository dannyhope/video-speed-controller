# Tester outreach — Video Speed Controller

> Living notes. Overwritten by `/usability-test recruit`. Last updated: 2026-08-23.

**Product:** Chrome extension to speed up or slow down video on almost any website.

**Who it’s for:** People who watch video in Chrome (YouTube, courses, talks, news) and want speed without hunting through each player’s menus.

**Stage:** Tryable on your machine (load unpacked). Not yet one-click for a friend — unlisted store link is the intended share path; the live dannyhope.co.uk page is still the privacy policy.

**How they try it (honest):**
- Best: you send the unlisted Chrome Web Store link, they install, they open a video they actually watch
- Until that link exists: sit with them, or put it on their Chrome yourself
- Load unpacked (`dist/` after `npm run pack:unpacked`) only for someone technical and willing

Aim: 3–5 people. Do not list shortcuts or tell them what to click.
