## Competitor signal

“Stoo” does not appear to be a recognised video-speed-controller competitor. The meaningful comparison set is:

- **Video Speed Controller**: broad compatibility, per-site rules, persistence, rewind/forward, and highly customisable shortcuts.
- **Global Speed**: speed plus audio/video effects, volume boost, pitch shift, background-media control, and URL rules.
- **Video Speed Controller Pro**: speed-memory, time-saved statistics, blacklist, and broad platform claims.

This project should not chase feature parity now. Its credible wedge is a quieter, privacy-first utility: keyboard-first control, a minimal Speed preferences link, custom presets, YouTube support, and caption-gap skipping.
